Overview

MKDWander Travel & Tours is a small travel agency in the Philippines that offers domestic and international tour packages. Before this app, the agency had no digital platform — all marketing and booking was done manually, making it hard to reach new clients.

This capstone project builds a Flutter-based mobile app that brings AI recommendations, AR navigation, predictive analytics, a chatbot, and a booking system into one branded platform. Instead of making travelers scroll through a catalog, the Smart Recommendations screen opens with a Virtual Travel Assistant card that says what it will do with their answers.

Pick your trip style and budget and I'll find the best tours for you!

What the app does

Seven things a traveler or agency staff can do.

Browse by island group

Home opens with a destination search and island-group chips, then rows of Promo Deals, Featured Packages and a Recommended For You pick.

Match by budget

Pick one or more island groups, set a budget tier (Budget ₱15k, Mid ₱20k–₱40k, Premium ₱50k+) and tap Find My Trips to get tours with a match percentage.

Read a tour at a glance

Each card shows the price per pax, trip length, package code and downpayment. Island Escape Palawan 2026 starts at ₱14,999 per pax with PHP 6,000 down.

Sign in quickly

Email or Google login via Firebase Authentication, a Register link, and a splash screen that syncs the profile so saved itineraries are ready when the app opens.

Navigate with AR

Point the phone camera at any destination and see real-time AR overlays of nearby landmarks, tourist spots, hotels, and restaurants powered by Google ARCore and Mapbox.

Get travel insights

The predictive analytics module shows crowd forecasts by month, best travel windows, and estimated costs based on MKDWander's historical booking data — so travelers can plan smarter.

Chat for support

An AI chatbot answers common questions about packages, pricing, and booking. Complex concerns are escalated to a human agent, with email fallback when the agent is offline.

User flow

  1. Splash

    The logo sits on a sunrise-yellow glow while a plane moves along a progress bar and the profile syncs via Firebase.

  2. Sign in

    A full-bleed waterfall photo with the tagline "Explore the world, one journey at a time." Email and Google buttons come first, with Register, Terms and Privacy Policy underneath.

  3. Home

    A sunset photo header holds the greeting, currency switch and search. Below it, island-group chips filter three rows: Promo Deals, Featured Packages and Recommended For You.

  4. Smart Match

    Choose Anywhere in PH, Luzon, Visayas or Mindanao, set a budget tier and tap Find My Trips. Results can be filtered by All, Day Tour, 3D-2N or 4D-3N, then booked or checked for available dates.

Design decisions

  • Price comes first. Every tour card leads with the starting price per pax, so travelers can rule options in or out quickly.
  • One set of regions. Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao are the same choices on Home and Smart Match, so the filters feel familiar.
  • Budget as named tiers. Budget, Mid and Premium with peso ranges are easier to pick than typing an amount.
  • Photography carries the mood. Destination photos fill the sign-in screen and the Home header, while cards and controls stay white and quiet.
  • Color has a job. Dark navy marks selected options, sunset orange marks badges and the budget slider, and blue or indigo marks actions like Find My Trips and Book Now.
  • AR as a natural extension. The navigation overlay uses the same card style as the rest of the app so the AR screen feels part of the same product, not a separate tool.
